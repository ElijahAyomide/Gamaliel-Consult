The firm

The law is our discipline.
People are our focus.

Gamaliel Consult is a legal practice built around a simple idea: good advice should make a complex situation easier to understand.

We work with businesses and individuals on matters that shape their future. Whether you are negotiating an agreement, managing a dispute or planning a significant change, we bring careful analysis and practical thinking to the conversation.

Our advice is grounded in your circumstances. We listen closely, explain the choices and work with you to identify a proportionate way forward.
