Making a will: where should you begin?

24 September 2026 · 6 min read · By Gamaliel Consult

Making a will is one of the most practical things you can do for the people you care about, yet many put it off because it is hard to know where to start. A little preparation makes the process much simpler.

Start with the people

Think about who you want to provide for, and whether anyone depends on you. If you have children, consider who you would trust to care for them. Think too about who should carry out your wishes as executor.

List what you own

Make a simple list of your main assets: property, savings, investments, business interests and personal items of value, along with any significant debts. It does not need to be exact, but it helps to see the whole picture.

Consider your circumstances

Blended families, business ownership, property in more than one place and beneficiaries who may need extra support can all affect how a will should be written. These are the situations where advice is especially valuable.

Make sure it is valid

A will usually needs to meet specific formal requirements, such as being in writing, signed and witnessed in the right way. A will that does not meet them may not be accepted, which can defeat your intentions.

Review it after life changes

Marriage, separation, the birth of a child, a move or a change in your finances are all good reasons to look at your will again. A regular review keeps it accurate.

This article provides general information only. The law and its application depend on the facts and jurisdiction. Please seek advice about your own circumstances.
