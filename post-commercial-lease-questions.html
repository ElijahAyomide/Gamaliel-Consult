Property & real estate

The questions to ask before taking a commercial lease

8 October 2026 · 7 min read · By Gamaliel Consult

A commercial lease can commit a business for years. The headline rent is only one part of the picture, and the obligations that sit around it often matter just as much. These are the questions worth asking before you commit.

How long is the term, and can you leave early?

Check the length of the term and whether there are break rights, when they can be used and what conditions apply. A lease that is too long can be as risky as one that is too short.

How is the rent set and reviewed?

Look at the starting rent, any rent-free period, and how and when the rent will be reviewed. Understand whether increases are fixed, tied to an index or based on market value.

What else will you have to pay?

Many leases require the tenant to contribute to service charges, building insurance, maintenance and taxes on top of the rent. Ask for a breakdown and for the recent history of these costs.

What are your repair and reinstatement duties?

Tenants are often responsible for keeping the property in good repair and for returning it in a particular condition at the end of the term. Costs at the end of a lease can be significant, so understand them now.

How may you use and change the space?

Confirm that the permitted use matches your plans, and check what consent you need for signage, alterations or sharing the space. Also ask whether you may assign or sublet if your needs change.

What security is required?

Landlords may ask for a deposit or a guarantee. Understand exactly what is being guaranteed, for how long, and who is personally liable.

This article provides general information only. The law and its application depend on the facts and jurisdiction. Please seek advice about your own circumstances.
