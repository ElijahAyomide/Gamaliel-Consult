Privacy policy

How we look after
your information.

What we collect through this website, why we collect it and the choices you have.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Gamaliel Consult (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we look after it.

1. Who we are

Gamaliel Consult is a legal practice. For the purposes of this policy we are the controller of the personal information you give us through this website. You can contact us at hello@gamalielconsult.com. Registered address: [Add registered office address].

2. What information we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us:

  • Enquiry form: your first and last name, email address, telephone number (optional), the practice area you are interested in, a brief outline of your matter and how you would prefer to meet.
  • Journal sign-up: your email address.
  • Emails you send us: your email address and anything you include in your message.

Please do not include confidential documents or sensitive personal information in your first enquiry. A short outline is enough to begin.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a conversation or meeting.
  • To carry out conflict checks before we can agree to act for you.
  • To send the journal, if you have signed up. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations and to keep our website secure.

We rely on your consent when you submit a form, our legitimate interest in running and protecting our practice, and our legal obligations where they apply. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Sending an enquiry does not make you a client

Submitting a form or sending us an email does not create a lawyer-client relationship. That only begins once we have completed a conflict check and agreed written terms with you.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only where it is needed to run the website and respond to you. This includes the providers that deliver our website forms and email, and our website hosting provider. These providers may only use your information to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

6. Cookies and third-party content

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To display text in our chosen typefaces, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

7. International transfers

Some of our service providers are based outside your country. Where information is transferred internationally, we take steps to make sure it remains protected in line with applicable data protection law.

8. How long we keep information

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are kept for [12 months] and then deleted. If we act for you, we keep your file for the period required by law and professional rules. Journal subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, to withdraw your consent, and to receive a copy in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email us at hello@gamalielconsult.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please take care about what you send us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
